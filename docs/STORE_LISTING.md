## Short description

Powerful, private video and audio controls with custom shortcuts, precise
seeking, site rules, and frame stepping.

## Long description

Take control of video and audio across the web.

StayFast Video gives playback power users fast, precise, and dependable controls
on sites that use HTML5 media. Change speed, seek, step through video frames,
set markers, and apply your preferred behavior without relying on each site's
limited player controls.

### Powerful playback control

- Adjust playback from 0.07× to 16×
- Create fully customizable keyboard shortcuts
- Rewind or advance by configurable amounts
- Step backward or forward one frame while paused
- Mark a position and return to it instantly
- Toggle preferred speeds with a single key

### Works the way you do

- Set preferred speeds for individual sites
- Remember your last-used speed
- Disable controls on selected sites
- Reposition and customize the on-video controller
- Reapply your chosen speed when a player tries to reset it

### Private by design

StayFast Video has no StayTech account, advertising, analytics, or external
service. Preferences remain in browser extension storage or, when enabled by
you, the browser vendor's settings-sync system. StayTech does not receive
browsing activity, page content, or playback history.

### Open and transparent

StayFast Video is an independently maintained StayTech edition of the
open-source Video Speed Controller project originally created by Ilya Grigorik.
Source code and complete license information are publicly available.

StayFast Video is developed and maintained by StayTech.
