Privacy policy

StayFast Video by StayTech · Updated October 16, 2026

Playback stays on your device

StayFast Video does not send personal information, browsing history, page content, playback history, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics to StayTech. The app and extension have no account, advertising, analytics SDK, or backend service.

Settings and website access

Safari stores your speed preferences, shortcuts, controller appearance, and website rules in extension storage on this device. These Safari preferences do not sync through iCloud. Website access lets the extension find media and apply your playback commands locally on the sites you allow. StayTech does not receive that page content or browsing activity.

Your choices

You can revoke website access or disable the extension in Safari settings. Reset the extension settings, clear its browser data, or uninstall the app to remove locally stored preferences. StayTech holds no playback or browsing data to delete.

Support and TestFlight

Support is optional. If you open the public GitHub tracker, GitHub handles that visit under its own policies. Information you choose to include in a support issue is visible on that public tracker; do not include private browsing details.

If you use an Apple TestFlight beta, Apple may provide StayTech with beta diagnostics and feedback you submit under TestFlight's terms. This is separate from the app's local playback processing. StayTech uses that information to investigate feedback and improve the beta; the app adds no independent telemetry service.

Questions and policy changes

Contact StayTech through the support tracker for privacy questions. Material policy changes will update this page and its date before any new collection is introduced.
