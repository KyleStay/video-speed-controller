StayFast Video

Make time for what you want to watch.

Make Safari your own

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions.
  2. Choose StayFast Video and turn on Allow Extension.
  3. Allow access to the websites where you want video controls.

Return to Safari and reload the video page. StayFast adds inline playback controls to supported videos. Tap Full screen to open StayFast’s player. Supported site Full screen buttons can open it too. Choose More → Apple player for Apple’s controls, or Use site player to restore the site’s player.

Ready when you are

Enable StayFast Video in Safari Settings → Extensions, then allow access to your video websites.

StayFast Video is enabled. Allow website access in Safari, then reload your video page.

StayFast Video is off. Turn it on in Safari Settings → Extensions.

Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions on iPhone/iPad, or Safari → Settings → Extensions on Mac.

A little more control

Change speed, skip back, or jump forward. Use the extension’s Settings for your preferred speeds, shortcuts, and website rules.

On iPhone and iPad, StayFast’s player offers precise scrubbing, speed, seek, and Lock/Unlock controls. Done returns to the page; More → Use site player restores the site’s controls. Safari’s browser bars may remain visible. Apple player and Picture in Picture use Apple’s controls; StayFast cannot change that interface. Higher playback speeds depend on the video and device.

Private by design

No account, analytics, or server. Preferences stay in this Safari installation and do not sync between devices. Website access lets StayFast find and control video and audio on the sites you allow.

Privacy policy Support

Open github.com/KyleStay/video-speed-controller/issues in Safari for support.

For iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 18.4 or later, and Mac with Safari 18.4 or later on macOS 15.4 or later.
